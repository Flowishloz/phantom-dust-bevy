## Ranked punch list

### 1. Cast/charge effects turn into a broken dark blob from the other split-screen camera — most visible bug (S/M, shared) — ✅ FIXED 2026-09-28

**Fixed** (render-only, `pd_game/src/effects.rs`/`fx.rs`; full root-cause writeup `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` "Camera-dependent 'dark blob' / milky orb"): the cause was confirmed, and it was rendering, not camera-facing math — the translucent puff emitters (the `90e7fd3e` pieces named as a candidate below) were drawn double-sided (`cull_mode: None`) with `AlphaMode::Blend`, which has no fixed draw order between a single mesh's own overlapping faces; the same piece could draw its 6 crossed-plane faces in a different order per camera, producing exactly this "same tick, two different results" bug. Switched those pieces (and the `solid` PDEFTOPT06/YKREFRECT00 pieces, same bug) to single-sided culling with a mode that needs no sort: `AlphaMode::Mask(0.5)` for the puffs (matches the disassembly's own "alpha-kill" reading of `PDEFTBB01`, already on record in `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` "Shaders"), `AlphaMode::Opaque` for the `solid` pieces (matches their disassembled alpha-1 output). Additive pieces (most of the effect) were untouched — pure addition is order-independent and was never the cause.

**Proof**: `PD_TEST_SPLIT=tb`, both cameras, same tick — `shots/vfxaudit/laser_tp057.png`/`slidelaser_tp050.png` (before: bottom view is an opaque dark-navy blob) vs. `shots/vfxaudit/after/laser_tp057.png`/`slidelaser_tp050.png` (after: both split views show the same clean glow) and `shots/vfxaudit/after/sheet_laser_before_after.png`/`sheet_slidelaser_before_after.png` (side-by-side montages). Not committed (game-derived, gitignored).

**Moves affected:** Laser, Slide Laser confirmed; likely every skill whose cast (slot 0) uses a sprite/puff emitter, i.e. most casts, since these were only recently un-hidden (`FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` "Cast/hit/impact sprite and puff emitters were being hidden," fixed 2026-09-27).

**What's wrong:** `sheet_slidelaser.png` (+833 ms column) and `sheet_laser.png` (+950 ms column), bottom view: from **one** player's camera the charge glow renders as an ugly, near-opaque dark teal/navy blob that swallows the character, while the **same instant from the other camera** (top view) looks like a reasonable (if oversized, see #2) glow. Same effect, same tick, two different and clearly-wrong-in-one-case results depending only on which camera is looking at it.

**Cause:** likely (not yet confirmed against the program) — an alpha-blended/additive billboard or trail quad (candidates: the `a07ac685`/`90e7fd3e` sprite/puff pieces, or the `b8de89ea` trail) not oriented or blended correctly at some camera angles, so what should be a soft additive glow instead shows its opaque backing/black texture fill from certain view directions. `PDEFTOPT05` screen-distortion pieces are already hidden by default (`FORMATS-EFFECTS.md`), so this isn't that.

**Fix:** `pd_game/src/effects.rs` — check billboard-facing math and blend state for the cast sprite/puff/trail pieces at grazing/reverse camera angles; reproduce with both `PD_TEST_SPLIT=sbs` and `=tb` from both camera positions. Size M given it isn't isolated to one skill.

### 2. Charge effects render as a big flat disc/ring, not a body-sized volumetric swirl (M, shared) — ✅ MOSTLY FIXED 2026-09-28 (lane-charge)

**Cause, confirmed against the effect data** (`cache/fx/eft0042.bounds.json`, `pd_convert effects` dumps of eft0042/eft0043/eft0053 — no code changed to get these, they're pure data reads): Laser's cast model's own rest-pose bounding box is `[-18, -1, -9]` to `[17, 31, 23]` — about 35 x 32 x 32 units — **before** its ~250 glow quads grow further over their own life (individual `grow` words up to +390%, e.g. Blaster's fist-charge quads add up to +8x their base size by the end of their window). `sim::BODY_HEIGHT` is 20 units. So the charge model is 1.5-3x a body's height/width at rest, and several times that at its largest moment — this alone explains both halves of the complaint: it "floats past the head and out past the shoulders" because it *is* bigger than the character, and it reads as legible rings rather than a blob because each spiral quad is individually large enough to be seen as its own shape instead of blending into a mass. Every one of these oversized pieces is placed directly at the character root (`joint: null`; `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` "Pieces are placed by their transform joint"), spread along spirals whose radius is baked into their own local geometry — unlike a joint-attached piece such as Psycho Knife's hand flash, which is inherently scoped to a bone and already reads correctly. Laurence's own footage (`ft_laser_beam_arc.png`) confirms the target: Laser's real charge glow stays a compact, torso-width swirl that never reaches past the shoulders.

**Fix** (`pd_game/src/effects.rs::animate_pieces`, render-only, `CAST_REACH`/`CAST_PIVOT_Y`, `PD_FX_CAST_REACH` overrides): a **not name-gated** rule — any slot-0, root-anchored (`joint: null`) piece that measures further than `CAST_REACH` (14 units, calibrated by eye) from the caster's chest is pulled in to that distance and shrunk by the same ratio, both size and spread together. A piece already inside the envelope passes through at ratio 1.0, untouched — this is why Psycho Wave and Psycho Knife's hand flash (both joint-attached, never reach this check) and, per a byte-for-byte screenshot check, Crystal Wall are unaffected, rather than being excluded by name. Confirmed this pass on Laser and Slide Laser (100% root-anchored) and on Blaster's own root-anchored pieces; Blaster also has `RHAND`-jointed charge quads at the fist this rule doesn't reach — if those are still individually oversized, that's a separate, unconfirmed follow-up (see `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md`).

**A regression was found and reverted in this same session, before merge**: an earlier iteration additionally redrew a 5-piece family on Slide Laser's cast (`eft0043` `m0_d4`..`m0_d8`, colour fading white/transparent → black/opaque) as an `AlphaMode::Mask` alpha-kill cutout, on the theory that it was the disassembled `PDEFTBB01` shader's real behaviour (per #1 above) and was the footage's missing green crescent. Orchestrator review of the resulting screenshots caught that this actually reproduced item #1's exact "dark blob" bug: because the piece's *colour*, not just its alpha, heads toward black as it becomes opaque, `Mask` drew it as a real, solid, dark quad over the caster. **Reverted** — that family is plain `AlphaMode::Add` again, like every other quad piece, which by construction can only go dim/invisible as its colour heads to black, never opaque-dark. Re-checked against the disassembly: word 9 (scale easing) *and* word 10 (colour easing) on this family are both `2` ("fast start" — an earlier read of word 10 as `0`/linear was a transcription error, corrected here), so under `Add` this family does still rise and fall within the piece's own 8-40 frame window, just not the way the footage's fast, blown-out flash does — see "Still off".

**Still off**: side-by-side against the kept footage frames (`sheet_slidelaser_vs_footage.png`, `sheet_laser_vs_footage.png` — built this pass specifically to compare directly, not just before/after each other) the charge is now believably closer to body-sized and no longer shows a dark blob, but Slide Laser's footage-specific "huge green crescent that blazes within ~150-300ms and fades by ~800ms" is still not reproduced as its own distinct sweep shape — ours reads as a size/brightness change in the existing diffuse swirl, not a directional crescent. Whatever draws that crescent in the original hasn't been identified with confidence this pass; the `m0_d4`..`m0_d8` family remains the leading candidate by position and timing, but its colour direction (fading toward black) argues against it being a simple, brighter additive piece, and reversing that reading without new evidence would be another guess. Flagged for a follow-up with fresh reverse-engineering budget, not chased further here. The general oversizing (this item's main complaint) is fixed for all three moves; Laser and Blaster are not known to have an equivalent crescent/flash element missing.

**Moves affected:** Laser, Slide Laser, Blaster, by the general reach-clamp rule (not by name). Confirmed unaffected: Psycho Wave, Crystal Wall (joint-attached or below the reach threshold).

**Proof**: `PD_TEST_PRESS=240,0,225`, `PD_TEST_SPLIT=tb`, `--stage st04 --near --loadout <id>,<id>,<id>,<id>` (Laser 65, Slide Laser 66, Blaster 80) with `PD_SHOT_TICKS=p0,p57` / `p0,p8,p14,p22,p29,p50` / `p0,p32,p78` — before/after pairs and montages in `shots/vfxaudit/charge/{before,after}/`, `shots/vfxaudit/charge/sheet_{laser,slidelaser,blaster}_before_after.png`, and the direct-against-footage sheets `sheet_slidelaser_vs_footage.png` / `sheet_laser_vs_footage.png` (footage frames cropped from the already-kept `ft_slide_laser_cast_and_fire.png` / `ft_laser_beam_arc.png`). Regression check: Psycho Wave (skill 1, `PD_SHOT_TICKS=p0,p10,p20`) and Crystal Wall (skill 26, `p0,p5`) rendered before/after — visually identical (`sheet_psychowave_before_after.png`, `sheet_crystalwall_before_after.png`), confirming the structural (not name-based) gate leaves both untouched. Not committed (game-derived, gitignored).

### 3. No floor/scene lighting from casts — LIGHT02 point lights are read but never drawn (M, shared, already-known gap) — ✅ FIXED 2026-09-28

**Moves affected:** Slide Laser and Blaster show it clearest (a wide green/orange wash lighting the floor and nearby walls around the caster in every footage frame); Psycho Knife's hit was already separately flagged in `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` (purple floor tint). Laser likely has the same emitter and is just harder to see against its blue-on-grey palette.

**What's wrong:** compare any "Foot" cell in `sheet_slidelaser.png`/`sheet_blaster.png` to the matching "Ours" cell — the footage's charge visibly bounces light onto the floor tiles and the pillar/wall behind the target; ours never lights anything outside the effect's own glow quads, so the floor and walls stay exactly as dark as the idle shot.

**Cause: confirmed** (`FORMATS-EFFECTS.md` "LIGHT02 (`893e3f31`) is a dynamic point light... Not drawn: our stages are baked and unlit ❓"; `GAME.md`/`FORMATS-STAGES.md` confirm stages are baked, unlit glTF + one directional light for characters, no per-effect dynamic lights).

**Fix:** either a real (cheap, effect-lifetime) point light spawned from the LIGHT02 emitter's colour/range/fade, or — cheaper and likely visually close enough given the stages are baked/unlit anyway — an emissive decal/soft quad projected on the floor under the caster in the emitter's colour. Size M; this is the single highest-leverage shared fix in this list (LIGHT02 appears in many effect files beyond the four moves checked here).

**Fixed 2026-09-28** (`pd_game/src/effects.rs`, render-only): went with the decal option — confirmed first that a real `PointLight` would be a no-op, since stages are baked into unlit vertex colours (`stage_look.rs`; `main.rs`'s `matte_characters` comment "Stage materials are unlit and untouched"). Added `effects::FxLight`: a flat, additive, soft-edged quad on the floor under the caster, coloured/sized/faded from the LIGHT02 emitter's own fields (colour word 12→13, radius word 0→1 × an unconfirmed `RANGE_SCALE` visual-calibration constant, alpha ramped by the word-9/word-10 fade envelope), capped at `MAX_LIGHT_DECALS` concurrent decals, `PD_LIGHT=0` to disable for A/B. One real bug hit and fixed along the way: the decal's world position was first computed by copying the barrier bubble's chest-height offset (`+ Vec3::Y * CHEST * 0.8`) from a different piece of code in `fx.rs` — wrong system; a cast's effect root is actually a child of the caster's own visual entity at local `Transform::IDENTITY`, i.e. already at the character rig's floor-level anchor, so subtracting a chest offset buried the decal underground. Fixed to use the root's own position directly (plus a small y epsilon).

**Proof**: tick-matched `PD_TEST_SPLIT=tb` sheets for Slide Laser and Blaster, both split-screen views, plus a pre-cast idle frame proving the stage is unchanged when no LIGHT02 slot is active — `engine/shots/vfxaudit/light/{slidelaser,blaster}_t*.png` (not committed, game-derived). `cargo test -p pd_formats -p pd_game -p pd_convert` stays green (315 total: 7 + 36 + 272), no new warnings.

**Still open:** the radius-to-world-unit scale (`RANGE_SCALE`) is a by-eye calibration against the footage, not read from the program's own light-table falloff — flagged unconfirmed in `FORMATS-EFFECTS.md`. The pre-existing "double atom ring"/dark-disc look from item 2 (Laser's own spiral-quad geometry, unrelated to this fix — reproduces identically with `PD_LIGHT=0`) still shows through the new floor glow in some frames and hasn't been touched here.

**Built, v1** (6028cac, lane-school): a camera-facing oval "hoop" quad behind each fighter. Rules right (dominant school colour, tie order, Level >= 1 gate, tests), look rejected by Laurence 2026-09-28 — the footage glow hugs the character's own silhouette, it isn't a ring.

**Built, v2** (lane-school, 2026-09-28, replaces the hoop; rules unchanged): `pd_game/src/school_aura.rs` + `school_aura.wgsl`, render-only. Each body mesh piece under a `PlayerVisual` gets a child "shell" entity sharing the same `SkinnedMesh` joints (so it poses with every animation frame, cloth included), drawn additively with a copy of the mesh whose normals are rebuilt; the vertex shader pushes the skinned surface out along those normals (`AURA_WIDTH_U`, thinner when the camera is close), the fragment shader shows only the shell's back faces with brightness `(-N·V)^AURA_FALLOFF` — a soft band just outside the silhouette, brightest against the body, fading to nothing outward. Weighted toward head and shoulders (ramp by height above the feet, `AURA_HEAD_BIAS`) and away from outstretched hands (fade off the body's vertical axis, `AURA_ARM_FADE_U`), as in the footage. All constants unconfirmed, tuned by eye against `shots/lockon/close.png` (env overrides `PD_AURA_WIDTH/FALLOFF/GAIN/HEAD/ARM/RIM` for tuning). Psycho's shade moved from pure red to the footage's pink-red.

**Root cause of the two earlier on-mesh attempts painting whole limbs solid** (inverted hull, per-fragment fresnel): not skinning — `pd_convert` writes every character vertex's `NORMAL` as the constant (0,0,1) (`pd_formats/src/character.rs`: the real shading normal is the game's object-space normal map, fed through a fixed T=X/B=Y/N=Z frame). After skinning, every vertex of a rigid limb shares one normal, so N·V is one flat value per limb (fresnel = limb painted solid) and "extrude along the normal" slides each limb sideways as a solid copy. v2 rebuilds smooth normals from the triangles at spawn (`shell_mesh`: area-weighted, welded by position so UV seams don't crack the shell, flipped by the mesh's signed volume so a mirrored winding can't turn it inside out; test `shell_normals_point_outward_either_winding`).
